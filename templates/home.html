Justice Data Platform

The new platform will bring together the tools and capabilities of the current Analytical Platform and Find MOJ data. It will provide everything you need to find, transform, analyse, and share data.

By design, it will be secure, high-performance, cost-effective, and aligned with data protection and governance policies.

You'll get a unified view of data across MOJ and be able to request access to data from different systems without moving or duplicating it. You'll also be able to store data securely and manage permissions on a project-by-project level, without needing GitHub access.

Find out what we've done so far, and what we're doing next, in our roadmap.

Manage data

Each business area will have its own data factory for ingestion, processing, storage, and sharing. The framework allows for clearer ownership, easier governance, and better, more consistent metadata.

Learn more about data factories

Access AI tools

Get access to a wide range of Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI tools through the AI Gateway API. Prevent fragmented deployments and reduce duplicated effort. Get structured governance and cost transparency across models.
